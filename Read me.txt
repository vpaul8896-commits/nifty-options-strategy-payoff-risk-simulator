# NIFTY Options Strategy Payoff & Risk Simulator

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel 2021 | Microsoft Power BI  
**Project Type:** Financial Modelling & Derivatives Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

The NIFTY Options Strategy Payoff & Risk Simulator is an Excel-based financial modelling tool integrated with Microsoft Power BI. It is designed to analyse the potential profit and loss of different NIFTY options strategies at expiry.

Users can enter NIFTY spot prices, option types, strike prices, premiums, positions and lot quantities. The simulator calculates individual option-leg P&L, combined strategy payoff, breakeven points and risk-reward metrics.

The project also includes scenario analysis and interactive dashboards to help users understand the risk and reward characteristics of options strategies.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Develop an Excel-based options payoff simulator.
- Analyse different NIFTY options strategies.
- Automate individual option-leg and combined P&L calculations.
- Calculate maximum profit, maximum loss and breakeven points.
- Evaluate risk and reward characteristics.
- Perform scenario analysis under different NIFTY price movements.
- Visualise financial results using Power BI.

## 6. Key Features

### Strategy Inputs
- Strategy selection through dropdown.
- NIFTY spot price and expiry date.
- Option type selection (Call/Put).
- Buy/Sell position selection.

- Combined strategy P&L.

## 7. Calculation Methodology

**Call Intrinsic Value**

MAX(Expiry Price - Strike Price, 0)

**Put Intrinsic Value**

MAX(Strike Price - Expiry Price, 0)

**Long Option P&L**

(Intrinsic Value - Premium) × Quantity

**Short Option P&L**

(Premium - Intrinsic Value) × Quantity

**Total Strategy P&L**

Sum of the P&L of all active option legs.

**Breakeven Point**

The expiry price at which the combined strategy P&L equals zero. The simulator identifies breakeven points using the calculated payoff values.

## 9. Power BI Integration

The Excel workbook is connected to Power BI through structured tables in the PowerBI_Data worksheet.

**Data tables:**
- `tblScenarioData`
- `tblRiskSummary`
- `tblPayoffCurve`

**Data workflow:**

1. Enter or modify strategy inputs in Excel.
2. Save the Excel workbook.
3. Open the connected Power BI report.
4. Select Home → Refresh.
5. Review the updated dashboard pages.

**Note:** Power BI visualises the output generated by the currently selected strategy in Excel. It does not independently calculate all 11 strategies simultaneously.

## 10. How to Use the Simulator

1. Open the Excel workbook.
2. Navigate to Strategy_Input.
3. Enter NIFTY spot price and expiry date.
4. Enter lot size and number of lots.
5. Select the required options strategy.
6. Enter option type, position, strike price and premium for each active leg.
7. Review results in Payoff_Calculation.
8. Review risk metrics in Risk_Analysis.
9. Analyse market movements in Scenario_Analysis.
10. Review the Excel Dashboard.
11. Save the workbook and refresh Power BI.

## 12. Assumptions and Limitations

- The simulator calculates options payoff at expiry only.
- It does not use live NIFTY market data or live option-chain prices.
- It does not calculate mark-to-market P&L before expiry.
- Option Greeks such as Delta, Gamma, Theta and Vega are not calculated.
- Implied volatility and time decay are not modelled.
- Brokerage, taxes, exchange charges and slippage are excluded.
- Payoff analysis is limited to the configured 80%-120% expiry-price range.
- Maximum and minimum P&L are based on the tested price grid and may not represent theoretical limits outside that range.
- The risk-free-rate input is included but is not used in the basic expiry-payoff formulas.
- Power BI displays the currently selected strategy's Excel-generated output.
